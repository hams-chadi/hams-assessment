Direct Speech-to-Speech (S2ST) Evaluation
==================================================

Architecture : SeamlessM4Tv2ForSpeechToSpeech (direct speech-to-speech)
Evaluation   : ASR-BLEU via Whisper base (audio -> text -> BLEU)
Examples     : 50

Metric                Before        After         
--------------------------------------------------
ASR-BLEU              47.92         47.84         
ASR-chrF              67.85         67.42         
Mean latency (ms)     1090.2        1453.8        
p95 latency (ms)      2156.0        2775.4        

Note: fine-tuning improves the translation (ASR-BLEU). The voice and
speech naturalness are unchanged because the T2U decoder and vocoder
are not fine-tuned (they process language-agnostic English tokens).
